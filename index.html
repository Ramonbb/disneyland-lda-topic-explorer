Exploring the Visitor Experience

An interactive LDA topic analysis of online reviews

Intertopic Distance Map (via multidimensional scaling)

About this visualization

Topics were identified using Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA). The map was reconstructed from the LDAvis visualization. For each selected topic, the 30 most relevant terms and three representative visitor reviews are displayed.

Select a topic on the map to explore its results.

Topic overview

Prevalence
i

Estimated percentage of tokens assigned to the topic.

Coherence (Cᵥ)
i

Topic coherence assessed using the Cᵥ metric (Röder et al., 2015), calculated with the coherence() function in the sentopics R package based on the ten highest-probability terms per topic.

Thematic representativeness
i

Percentage of sentences whose probability of belonging to the topic exceeds the mean of the maximum topic probabilities.

Top 30 relevant terms

i

Relevance values of terms were transformed using Exp(Relevance) to facilitate their visualization on a positive scale, without altering their relative ranking within each topic.
