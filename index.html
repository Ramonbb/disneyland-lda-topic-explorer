Topic overview

Prevalence
i

Estimated percentage of tokens assigned to the topic.

Coherence (Cᵥ)
i

Topic coherence was assessed using the Cᵥ metric, calculated with the coherence() function of the sentopics R package using the ten highest-probability terms per topic.

Thematic representativeness
i

Percentage of sentences whose probability of belonging to the topic exceeds the mean of the maximum topic probabilities.

i
